Ink UI Kit

A one-colour component kit for apps that talk to e-paper displays. Pure black on warm off-white, a heavy 2px border as the signature, pill controls, and a panel component that renders like a 1-bit screen. Drop in ink-ui-kit.css and use the class names shown below.

1

Colors

There is no accent hue. Hierarchy comes entirely from value: black for action and emphasis, three greys for text, two for lines, one off-white ground.

--ink-black#111111 · text, fills, active
--ink-900#1a1a1a · heavy borders
--ink-700#444444 · secondary text
--ink-500#8a8a8a · captions, eyebrows
--ink-300#d6d6d6 · hairlines
--ink-200#ececec · soft dividers
--ink-100#f5f5f3 · page ground
--ink-white#ffffff · surfaces, paper

Typography

Three faces with three jobs. A tight grotesk carries the interface, a serif is reserved for quoted text on the panel, and a mono handles anything numeric.
